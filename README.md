A 100-word villain monologue — a dark lord who raised a tower, burned three towns, and now cannot quite keep his place in his own doom speech:

> Again? Third young hero this week. Sit. No—stand. Heroes stand, I... where was I? Right. Doom. I raised this tower, stone by stone, burned three proud towns, taught the crows my name. Now the seal is cracked, the orb is—sorry, the orb is lit, the dead are up, late, but up. You brought a sword? Good. Good. Mine is... somewhere. Ask the goblin. He files things. He files everything. Prophecies, mostly. Yours said dawn. It is dawn, I think. The bells... never mind. Kneel, and I will—wait. Let me just sit first. Then doom, at long last. Yours, not mine.

The program writes that monologue (exactly 100 words, 554 characters) to `/output/entry.txt` for make.fun's "The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue" bounty. The tiredness lives in the pauses, repetitions and deflections — it is never announced. The program self-checks the 1–600 character gate and the exact word count before writing, then reads the file back to verify it landed byte for byte.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
